# Design

<!-- impeccable:design-schema 1 -->

## Visual World

SAVVA uses a “night arrival” visual world: the site should feel like approaching the glowing SAVVA storefront in Madinah after sunset, seeing warm light, wood, plants, street seating, and a clear invitation to enter. The page should not look like a generic beige cafe template; it should feel like a real place with a threshold, a route, a menu board, and a quiet interior.

## Mode

Persuade. The visitor should decide to visit, open the route, view the menu, or call within seconds.

## Direction Contract

### First viewport

The first viewport must behave like an arrival scene. The real exterior photo leads the page, the brand name is large and calm, and the practical actions sit close to the promise: route, menu, reservation. Proof points should read like a compact arrival board, not decorative metrics.

### Materials

- Deep olive-black night surfaces.
- Warm storefront light, amber highlights, and porcelain text.
- Wood-brown accents taken from the cafe frames and facade.
- Thin illuminated rules instead of heavy cards.
- Real exterior and interior photos as the main visual authority.

### Typography

Display typography should feel editorial and premium through scale, spacing, and contrast. Body text stays clean and readable in Arabic, English, and Russian. Do not use overly playful or generic AI-style wording.

### Composition

Use fewer, larger moments. The page should alternate between cinematic photo-led sections and precise practical information. Menu content should feel like a curated cafe board with clear prices, not a spreadsheet.

### Interaction and Motion

Motion should be quiet and purposeful: slight reveal, soft light movement, and tactile button states. No scattered animations or decorative effects that slow down the visitor.

### Responsive Rules

Mobile is primary. Header, language switcher, hero copy, proof points, menu panels, and bottom CTA must never overlap or create horizontal scrolling. The first actions must stay reachable without hunting.

## Signature Pattern

Use “arrival strips”: narrow horizontal or vertical information bands that combine location, proof, and action. They echo signage, street wayfinding, and a cafe menu board.

## Boundaries

Preserve real cafe facts, menu prices, real user-provided photos, address, phone, Google Maps link, and three-language behavior. Do not introduce fake photography, fake reviews, invented awards, or unconfirmed claims.
